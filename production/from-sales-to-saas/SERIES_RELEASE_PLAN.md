# 3. EPISODE RELEASE PLAN

Target: one episode per day, in the order below.

The daily cadence is a publishing target, not permission to publish without approval. Build a small buffer where possible. Do not sacrifice accuracy or QA just to meet a calendar date. Andrew approves every final episode before publication.

| Release order | Episode | Working title | Target duration | Core question |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | EP01 | From Sales to SaaS: Why I Started Building | 7:00 | How did a salesperson become a software builder? |
| 2 | EP02 | Find a Problem Worth Solving | 6:00 | How do you know a problem is worth solving? |
| 3 | EP03 | Validate Before You Build | 6:30 | Why is interest not the same as demand? |
| 4 | EP04 | Scope the MVP: Build What Matters | 7:00 | What is the smallest useful product? |
| 5 | EP05 | Building Software With Claude and AI | 10:00 | How do you use AI without handing over your judgement? |
| 6 | EP06 | Ship It: Hosting, Databases, Payments and Failed Deploys | 7:00 | What happens between working code and a usable product? |

| 9 | EP09 | Your First 30 Days as a SaaS Founder | 3:30 | What should a new founder actually do first? |

**Total planned runtime: 60:00.**

EP01 combines the original cold open (M0, 1:30) and founder journey (M1, 5:30). The remaining original modules each become their own episode.

## Publishing sequence

- Publish EP01 only after Andrew has reviewed the visual edit, recorded voice-over, and approved the final export.
- Prepare EP02's production folder and brief while EP01 is under review, but do not start EP02's full visual production until approval is given.
- Once the workflow is established, aim to publish one finished episode each day.
- Keep a production buffer where practical. If an episode is not ready, delay it rather than publish an unreviewed or inaccurate cut.
- Create three vertical short-form cuts from each episode where the source material supports them.
- Do not schedule, upload, publish, or distribute anything without Andrew's explicit approval.
