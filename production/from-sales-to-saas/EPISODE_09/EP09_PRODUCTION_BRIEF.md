## EP09 — Your First 30 Days as a SaaS Founder (3:30)

Core question: What should a new founder actually do first?

Cover:
- Choose one audience.
- Find and understand a real problem.
- Interview people about past behaviour.
- Test the problem before building.
- Scope the smallest useful product.
- Use AI with review and testing.
- Get feedback and start distribution.
- Review evidence at day 30.

Visuals:
- 30-day roadmap.
- Weekly milestones.
- Problem validation checklist.
- MVP scope card.
- Build/test/distribute loop.
- Strong Andrew Builds end card.
